# CTC CREATOR LICENSE AGREEMENT

**Draft 1.0 — October 20, 2026**

This Creator License Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into between **Change To Come ("CTC")**, operator of the CTC / Cut The Check platform, and the undersigned creator ("Creator"), effective on the date of acceptance (the "Effective Date").

CTC is an infrastructure layer connecting Creators, Content, Consumers, and Commerce across third-party platforms ("Channels"). CTC is not a social network, streaming service, or wallet provider competing with the Channels; each Channel remains a separate service governed by its own terms.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Content"** — all audio, video, live broadcasts, images, artwork, text, and other material Creator submits, uploads, or makes available through CTC.
- **"Channels"** — the third-party platforms connected through CTC, including YouTube, TikTok, Spotify, Apple, Instagram, Facebook, Twitch, Kick, and other supported services.
- **"Services"** — CTC's platform functions: Create, Connect, Certify, Distribute, Insight, Monetize, and Wallet.

## 2. Grant of License

Creator grants CTC a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-bearing license** to host, reproduce, distribute, publicly perform and display, promote, and monetize the Content through the Services and across the Channels, solely to operate and promote the Services. This includes the right to:

- format, transcode, and excerpt the Content as reasonably required by each Channel, including short-form clips used to promote the Content;
- make the Content available simultaneously on all selected Channels, or on a staggered schedule, **at Creator's direction** (Section 4);
- sublicense the Content to Channels and distribution partners solely as needed to carry out the distribution and monetization above.

**No exclusive rights are granted under this Agreement.** Any exclusive license, buyout, or transfer of rights requires a separate written agreement signed by Creator.

## 3. Ownership

**Creator retains 100% of the copyright and all ownership rights in the Content.** Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of the Content, Creator's name or likeness (beyond promotional use of the Content itself), or Creator's other intellectual property to CTC. CTC acquires only the license stated in Section 2.

## 4. Distribution — Creator Controls the Release

Creator chooses how Content is released: **on all selected Channels at the same time, or staggered on Creator's own timeline**, unless a specific release campaign agreed in writing sets a different schedule. Creator may add or remove Channels for future releases at any time through the Services.

## 5. Monetization and Revenue

- CTC will monetize the Content through available revenue sources, which may include subscriptions, tips, sponsorships and brand deals, advertising, licensing and sync placements, merchandise, digital products, and affiliate revenue.
- **Revenue is tracked and reported separately for each Channel**, based on Creator's performance on that Channel, and is visible to Creator through CTC Insight.
- Creator is paid the revenue actually received by CTC that is attributable to Creator's Content, less the fees for Creator's CTC plan as disclosed in the Services at the time of the transaction. Amounts are held and paid out through **CTC Wallet** (USD; cryptocurrency options, where offered, are provided through regulated partners).
- Creator is responsible for taxes on amounts paid to Creator and for providing accurate payout and tax information.

## 6. Sponsorships, Brand Deals, and Sync Licensing

CTC may present Creator with sponsorship, brand, and licensing opportunities. Non-exclusive placements under existing deal terms may proceed under this Agreement. **No exclusive sponsorship, category lockout, or exclusive sync license binds Creator without Creator's separate written approval of that deal's terms.**

## 7. Certification

Creator agrees to complete CTC Certify identity and content verification as reasonably required for distribution, monetization, and payout. Creator will keep account and payout information accurate and current.

## 8. Creator Representations

Creator represents and warrants that: (a) Creator owns or controls all rights in the Content needed to grant this license; (b) the Content does not infringe any third party's rights; (c) Creator has the authority to enter this Agreement; and (d) the Content complies with applicable law and each Channel's policies.

## 9. Channel Terms

Distribution to each Channel is also subject to that Channel's own terms and policies. If a Channel removes or restricts Content under its policies, CTC will notify Creator through the Services where reasonably possible. CTC does not control Channel decisions.

## 10. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated. Either party may terminate at any time through the Services or by written notice. On termination: CTC will stop new distribution of the Content and request removal from Channels within a commercially reasonable time; licenses already granted to Channels for Content distributed before termination survive as required by those Channels' terms; and **all accrued, unpaid earnings attributable to Creator will be paid out** in the ordinary course.

## 11. Liability and Indemnity

Creator agrees to indemnify CTC against third-party claims arising from the Content or Creator's breach of Section 8. CTC's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the amounts paid or payable to Creator in the twelve months before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages.

## 12. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Kentucky. It is the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Content license and supersedes prior discussions on that subject. CTC may update this Agreement with notice through the Services; continued use after notice constitutes acceptance of the updated terms for Content distributed afterward. If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder continues in effect.
